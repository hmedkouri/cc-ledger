# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

* being kind and assuming good faith;
* accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and giving it kindly;
* focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it;
* being precise about technical disagreements rather than personal ones.

Behaviour that does not:

* sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
* trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
* public or private harassment;
* publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without explicit permission.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub, either
by direct message or via a private security advisory on this repository. Reports
will be reviewed and investigated, and will be handled in a way that respects the
privacy and safety of whoever reported the incident.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
